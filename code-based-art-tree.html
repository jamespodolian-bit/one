Code-Based Art

▼
Code-Based Art
Art defined strictly by software logic, algorithms, and code execution
▼
Algorithmic & Pure Math Art
Visual expressions derived from mathematical equations and computational sequences
▼
Fractal Art
Visuals characterized by infinite self-similarity derived from mathematical formulas like the Mandelbrot set
▼
Vector-Based Art
Mathematical formulas generating lines, points, and curves that scale infinitely without pixelation
▼
Simulation & Autonomous Systems
Systems that evolve and generate patterns through rule-based interactions over time
▼
Cellular Automata
Grid-based systems where cells evolve based on the state of their neighbors, like Conway's Game of Life
▼
Agent-Based / Swarm Art
Systems where independent "agents" move and interact based on rules like flocking and steering behaviors
▼
Visual Manipulation & Rendering
Direct manipulation of pixels, rendering pipelines, and digital data for aesthetic effect
▼
Pixel / Grid-Based Art
Algorithms that manipulate individual pixel matrices to form structured, retro, or mosaic-like visuals
▼
Shader Art
Highly optimized, math-heavy GLSL/HLSL code executed directly on the GPU for real-time fluid animations
▼
Glitch Art
The intentional corruption of digital data, file code, or rendering logic to introduce aesthetic digital artifacts
